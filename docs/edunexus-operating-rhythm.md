# EduNexus Operating Rhythm v1.0

## Purpose

The EduNexus Operating Rhythm defines the recurring cadence through which the company learns, builds, serves schools, and improves.

It exists to ensure that progress becomes habitual rather than dependent on motivation.

The rhythm should remain lightweight enough for a solo founder while naturally expanding as the company grows.

---

# Daily Rhythm

## Morning

Review Founder Companion.

Identify today's single highest-leverage outcome.

Review blockers.

Review scheduled school interactions.

Review Build Guardian if coding.

Begin with the most important work.

---

## During the Day

Capture observations immediately.

Do not trust memory.

Record:

Customer observations.

Engineering discoveries.

Questions.

Decisions.

Unexpected events.

---

## End of Day

Review:

What moved the company forward?

What was learned?

What remains open?

Prepare tomorrow's first task.

End deliberately.

---

# Weekly Rhythm

Every Monday

Mission Control.

Choose no more than three weekly outcomes.

Review current sprint.

Review outreach commitments.

Identify major risks.

---

Every Friday

Learning Review.

Answer:

What surprised us?

What did schools teach us?

What assumption changed?

What improved?

What should stop?

Update:

Voice of Customer.

Sales Playbook.

Decision Journal.

Market Intelligence if relevant.

Celebrate one meaningful win.

---

# Monthly Rhythm

Review:

Company Roadmap.

Growth metrics.

Pilot health.

Engineering progress.

Sales process.

Customer success.

Founder workload.

Financial health.

Adjust priorities.

Do not rewrite strategy unless evidence demands it.

---

# Quarterly Rhythm

Review:

Operating Manifesto.

DNA Test.

Constitution.

Decision Journal.

Market Intelligence.

Company Roadmap.

Determine:

Have we earned the right to enter the next phase?

If not, strengthen the current one.

---

# Twice Each Year

Review:

Futures Lab.

Strategic Bets.

Weak Signals.

Scenario Planning.

Ask:

What future deserves more preparation?

What future deserves less attention?

---

# Annual Rhythm

Review the entire company.

Ask:

What capabilities were gained?

What trust was earned?

Which systems no longer serve us?

What should be simplified?

What should remain unchanged?

Publish an Annual Reflection.

---

# Rhythm Rules

The rhythm exists to reduce decision fatigue.

Missing a review occasionally is acceptable.

Abandoning the rhythm entirely is not.

Meetings should never exist simply because the calendar says so.

Every review must improve understanding or execution.

Otherwise remove it.

---

# Scaling

As EduNexus grows:

Daily rhythm remains personal.

Weekly rhythm becomes team-based.

Monthly rhythm becomes cross-functional.

Quarterly rhythm becomes strategic.

The structure remains familiar even as participants change.

---

# Final Principle

Consistency compounds.

Small reviews conducted faithfully over years create greater wisdom than occasional heroic planning sessions.

The rhythm exists so EduNexus continually learns, improves, and serves schools without losing focus on its mission.

#### Chapter 1 — "The Birth of Educational Intelligence Engineering"

**Location:** §1.1–1.9, lines 34–160.
**Constitutional Traceability:** This chapter is the direct historical ancestor of Axioms 3, 4, 8, 9, 10 — its "six properties" (Temporality, Causality, Multiplicity of Stakeholders, Institutional Context, Longitudinal Accountability, Ethical Weight) are a proto-version of the audited axiom set, arrived at independently before the axioms existed. Its "five architectural concerns" map to Blueprint Ch.2 (2.1–2.7). Strengthens the discipline-identity argument the Preface must make.
**Verdict: REFORGE.**
**Reasoning:** The underlying insight — that education has irreducible properties no borrowed domain shares — is exactly right and is *why* the Foundational Axioms exist. But this chapter predates the axioms and duplicates their content in unaudited form: e.g. "Ethical Weight" here is a single paragraph where Axiom 10 required a full audit pass (developmental framing, the two absorbed corollaries) to survive as domain-specific rather than generic. Keeping this chapter's prose as-is would silently reintroduce claims the audit already tightened or rejected (e.g., §1.4's "distinct tradeoffs" list is assertion without the audit's rigor). The five architectural concerns section (§1.3) also uses a "Learner Model / Intelligence Layer" vocabulary that must be remapped to the constitutional terms (Projection, Reasoning Engine).
**Required Actions:** Do not reuse §1.1's six-properties list directly — Ch.1 of the new manuscript must *derive* the axioms fresh (per the blueprint's explicit instruction that they be earned, not inherited by assertion), using this section only as a source of good classroom-adjacent phrasing, not as the argument's skeleton. §1.2's definitional work ("what EIE is not" — vs. EdTech/LMS/ERP/AIEd/Adaptive Learning/Learning Analytics) is strong, reusable, largely unclaimed by the current blueprint — recommend REFORGE into the Preface's "why this book exists" / field-positioning section, lightly touched. §1.5's "Learner Is Not a User" and "Intelligence Is Not AI" are strong, quotable claims — extract as Design Heuristics (see Pattern Extraction). §1.7 "Common Mistakes" is proto-Anti-Pattern material.
**Dependencies:** Must be reforged *after* Ch.1's axiom-derivation prose exists, so terminology and claims are checked against the frozen (not draft) axioms.
**Pattern Extraction:**
- *Design Heuristic:* "The Learner Is Not a User" — systems that treat learners as transactional users optimize for engagement, not learning; this corrupts the data model, not just the product. (Origin: §1.5.1)
- *Design Heuristic:* "Intelligence Is Not AI" — a rule-based system that correctly identifies a gap is more intelligent, in the relevant sense, than an LLM producing confident, pedagogically wrong text. (Origin: §1.5.3; direct forerunner of Blueprint 3.1 and Axiom 6/Traceable Derivation.)
- *Anti-Pattern candidate:* "LMS-as-Starting-Point" — adopting LMS architecture and adding intelligence as an afterthought (§1.7). Needs a Corrected-Pattern cross-reference to Blueprint 2.1–2.2 once drafted.
**Vocabulary Updates:** `Learner Model` → must resolve to **Projection** (or **Capability profile**, context-dependent) per Blueprint glossary; `Intelligence Layer` → **Reasoning engine**; `Event Stream` → **Evidence** (the archive's "Event Stream" bundles what the Constitution splits into raw Evidence ingestion vs. derived Projection — flag as a REFORGE-time disambiguation, not a straight rename).

#### Chapter 3 — "Domain-Driven Design for Education"

**Constitutional Traceability:** Directly and heavily supports Blueprint 2.1 (Domain-Driven Design for Learning Systems) — this chapter is the most mature, most directly reusable material found so far. **Verdict: KEEP with terminology remapping** (§3.2 ubiquitous language table, §3.3 bounded contexts, §3.4 context mapping, §3.6 domain events), **REFORGE** (§3.5 aggregates — needs Axiom 3/Trajectory and Axiom 2/Confidence integration it doesn't yet have), **DISCARD** (§3.7 value objects — implementation-guide granularity, not discipline-defining).
**Reasoning:** This is the strongest chapter reviewed so far against the six audit tests applied in spirit: it is timeless (DDD's bounded-context concept doesn't age), derives from a genuine SE-principle correctly adapted (not generically imported — the Anticorruption-Layer-as-domain-concern argument in §3.4.3, "0.73 risk score" → "Amina may need support," is a precise, non-generic illustration of Axiom 6/Traceable Derivation feeding Blueprint 3.4's view-adapters), and teaches the reader to think (bounded-context reasoning) rather than merely what to build. The `Intelligence Context`'s stated invariant "every recommendation is traceable to the evidence that generated it" (§3.3.5) is, verbatim, Axiom 6 — this predates the axiom and should be cited as the concrete architectural mechanism the axiom demands. §3.5 (aggregates) is good DDD but the Learner aggregate as specified doesn't yet carry an ECM-banded confidence field or explicit trajectory representation — needs rebuilding to conform, not just relabeling. §3.7 (value objects) is correct DDD instruction but is implementation pattern-library material more appropriate for a companion reference than a masterclass narrative chapter — the discipline is not weakened by removing it (fails the "would removing this weaken the discipline" test).
**Required Actions:** Remap `Intelligence Context` → **Reasoning Engine** context; retitle its "Learner Context" invariants using ECM band language (`Evidence records are immutable` stays, but competency records need explicit `Band[claim]` notation added). The context-mapping patterns (§3.4) are excellent Engineering Pattern candidates for Appendix D as-is, once renamed to constitutional context names. §3.6's event schemas should be checked against Axiom 5's "batch supersession" requirement — as currently written they support single-record correction implicitly but don't name batch supersession; extend, don't discard.
**Dependencies:** Feeds Blueprint 2.1 directly; §3.6 events feed 2.2 (Evidence Pipeline); §3.4.3's ACL example is the single best available worked illustration for Blueprint 2.6 (Explainability as Enforceable Contract) — flag as a priority REFORGE target, high value.
**Pattern Extraction:**
- *Engineering Pattern:* **Anticorruption Translation of Intelligence Output** — Name: ACL-as-Domain-Translation. Problem: raw reasoning-engine output (scores, probabilities) is meaningless or harmful shown directly to non-technical stakeholders. Forces: fidelity to underlying evidence vs. accessibility of language. Solution: a dedicated context-boundary translation layer, treated as domain logic, not UI formatting. Consequences: adds a translation-maintenance burden but is the only way to satisfy Axiom 8 (Plurality) without duplicating truth per audience. Related: Stakeholder View-Adapter (Blueprint 3.4). (Origin: §3.4.3.)
- *Engineering Pattern:* **Customer-Supplier Evidence Contract** — the consuming context (Learner/Evidence) dictates the evidence format; the producing context (Assessment) must conform. Prevents evidence-schema drift. (Origin: §3.4.2.)
- *Design Heuristic:* "Generic CRUD events (`student_updated`) instead of meaningful domain events (`CompetencyMasteryAchieved`)" as a named common mistake — pairs directly with Axiom 6. (Origin: §3.10.)
**Vocabulary Updates:** `Intelligence Context` → **Reasoning Engine** (bounded context housing it); `bounded context` itself is already a constitutional term (Axiom 9 audit note, Blueprint glossary) — no change needed, this chapter is the term's best-developed source material.

#### Chapter 4 — "Modeling Learning"

**Location:** §4.1–4.9, lines 651–893.
**Constitutional Traceability:** Directly strengthens Axiom 2 (Bounded, Derived Confidence) and Axiom 3 (Trajectory over Snapshot) with the most technically detailed material found so far on *how* to compute a capability estimate — feeds Blueprint 2.4 (ECM) and 2.5 (Capability and Risk Models) concretely. §4.7 ("Failure Modes") is a direct, independently-arrived-at instance of the Five Failure Questions discipline (§10 of the blueprint), applied to the learner-model component specifically before that discipline existed as a named method.
**Verdict: REFORGE** (§4.2 knowledge dimension, §4.6 mastery models — valuable but must be rebuilt in ECM band vocabulary, not the archive's ad hoc `confidence: Float [0.0,1.0]` scalar, which Axiom 2/ECM explicitly forbids), **KEEP with light edit** (§4.7 Failure Modes — needs only relabeling into the Five Failure Questions format), **REFORGE** (§4.4 cold start, §4.5 validation — good content, wrong vocabulary), **DISCARD** (§4.3's specific graph-database engineering-cost commentary — implementation detail, not principle).
**Reasoning:** This chapter is where the archive's material comes closest to *contradicting* the frozen Constitution and must therefore be rebuilt, not merely edited: §4.2.2's `CompetencyRecord` schema literally specifies `confidence: Float [0.0, 1.0]` — exactly the "invented percentage" the ECM's audit (§11.2, Axiom 2) rules out by design. This is not a minor terminology gap; it is a direct architectural contradiction, and per the Constitution's own authority ordering, the archive is wrong here, not the Constitution. The underlying intent (evidence-weighted, recency-aware, multi-context competency tracking) is exactly right and maps closely to ECM's four inputs (Source Reliability, Recency, Corroboration, Volume) — §4.4.2 "Evidence Quality" independently names three of ECM's four inputs (construct validity≈reliability signal, reliability, recency, volume) without the fourth (corroboration/independent-source-agreement), and without the banding mechanism. This is a near-miss: REFORGE by replacing the scalar with `Band[claim]` notation and adding the missing corroboration input, not by discarding the underlying evidence-quality reasoning, which is sound. §4.6's Mastery Model schema is similarly a near-miss — its `evidence_requirements` block (minimum count, required contexts, consistency threshold, recency requirement) is essentially ECM's band-entry-criteria table in embryonic form and should be REFORGED directly into Blueprint 2.4/2.5's worked schema example, a strong candidate for **KEEP-the-structure, REFORGE-the-fields**. §4.7's four failure modes (Score Trap, Recency Trap, False Precision Trap, Missing Data Trap) map almost one-to-one onto what the Five Failure Questions discipline would produce for the capability-projection component — high-value, low-effort REFORGE.
**Required Actions:** Rebuild §4.2.2's schema replacing the confidence scalar with ECM bands; add the missing Corroboration input explicitly. REFORGE §4.6's Mastery Model schema as a primary worked example for Blueprint 2.5. Relabel §4.7's four traps using the Five Failure Questions template (what fails / why / detection / correction / trust restoration) rather than leaving them as a flat list — currently they answer "what" and implicitly "why" but never address detection/correction/trust-restoration, which the Constitution now requires.
**Dependencies:** Must follow ECM's full spec (Blueprint 2.4) being drafted, since every schema here needs the band vocabulary retrofitted.
**Pattern Extraction:**
- *Failure Pattern:* **The Score Trap** — Layer: Projection. What can fail: multidimensional competency reduced to one number. Why: pressure for simplicity/comparability. Detection: check whether any UI or API exposes a bare numeric competency score with no evidence trail. Correction: replace with ECM-banded, evidence-linked representation. Trust restoration: requires explaining to affected stakeholders that prior comparisons made on the score were not meaningful — a genuinely hard case for Axiom 10's trust-repair corollary, worth flagging in Ch.4.4's consolidated failure catalog. (Origin: §4.7.)
- *Failure Pattern:* **The False Precision Trap** — reporting "84.3% mastery" implies precision the underlying measurement cannot support; direct violation of Axiom 2. (Origin: §4.7.)
- *Failure Pattern:* **The Missing Data Trap** — absence of recent evidence should decay confidence (Axiom 2/ECM §11.5 decay), not silently preserve a stale value. This archive section independently discovered the need for ECM's decay mechanism. (Origin: §4.7.)
- *Design Heuristic:* "The knowledge graph is the appropriate data structure for the competency dimension" (§4.3) — direct precursor to Axiom 7/Structured Knowledge.
**Vocabulary Updates:** `confidence: Float [0.0,1.0]` → **`Band[claim]`** (ECM notation, §11.6) everywhere it appears in schemas, no exceptions — this is the archive's most important single vocabulary correction. `Mastery Model` stays as a term but its `evidence_requirements` block should be explicitly cross-referenced as "this is what a Confidence Band's entry criteria look like in a worked schema."

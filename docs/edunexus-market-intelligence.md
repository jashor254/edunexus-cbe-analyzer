# EduNexus Market Intelligence Program

## Purpose

The EduNexus Market Intelligence Program exists to build a continuously improving understanding of the Kenyan education ecosystem.

The objective is not simply to know the market.

The objective is to make better strategic decisions because EduNexus understands schools better than competitors do.

---

# Mission

Create the most evidence-based understanding of the Kenyan school technology market.

Every important strategic decision should become easier because the company understands:

Schools.

Teachers.

Principals.

Parents.

County differences.

Curriculum.

Policy.

Technology adoption.

Budgets.

Competitors.

# Market Hypotheses

Maintain a list of assumptions.

Examples:

"Principals care more about reporting than AI."

"Teachers value time savings over automation."

"County referrals outperform cold outreach."

Each hypothesis should have:

Evidence supporting it.

Evidence contradicting it.

Confidence level.

Next validation step.

---

# Opportunity Register

Maintain an evolving list of:

Emerging needs.

New partnerships.

Grant opportunities.

Teacher communities.

Education conferences.

County events.

Policy windows.

Strategic alliances.

Rank by:

Impact.

Feasibility.

Timing.

Strategic alignment.

---

# Quarterly Market Review

Every quarter answer:

What changed?

What surprised us?

Which assumptions failed?

Which opportunities appeared?

Which competitors changed?

Which technologies matter now?

Which trends are noise?

What should EduNexus do differently?

---

# Decision Support

Before any major strategic decision ask:

What market evidence supports this?

What evidence contradicts it?

What remains unknown?

How can we validate cheaply before committing?

---

# Success Metric

Success is not measured by the size of this report.

Success is measured by how often it prevents poor strategic decisions and reveals opportunities earlier than competitors.

---

# Final Rule

The market is not static.

Neither is EduNexus.

The Market Intelligence Program exists to ensure the company continually adapts through observation, validation, and learning rather than assumptions.
